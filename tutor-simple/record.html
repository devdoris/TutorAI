Your own voice

Teach in your voice

The tutor plays a file for every line it says. Record those lines yourself and it teaches in your voice instead — no model, no training, nothing sent anywhere. Lines you have not recorded fall back to the Nigerian voice, so you can do a few at a time.

Before you start
  1. Somewhere quiet. A headset microphone beats a laptop one.
  2. Read the line as if a child is in front of you. A short pause before and after is fine — silence is trimmed automatically.
  3. Press Record the lot to go line by line without touching the mouse: it records, saves, and moves on.
  4. Then pick your name in the Voice picker on the Board.

This is your recording played back, not a model imitating you, so it always sounds exactly like you and needs nothing but this laptop. A cloned voice — one that could read a line you never recorded — is a separate job: it needs a speech model, and these same recordings are what it would learn from. Twenty or thirty lines is already enough reference audio for that later.
