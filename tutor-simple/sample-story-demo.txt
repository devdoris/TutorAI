Luna woke up before sunrise and stepped into the garden with a basket in her hands. She found a tiny fox curled beneath the lemon tree, trembling beside a silver bell. When the bell jingled, Luna smiled and followed the sound down the lane, past the bakery, and across the old bridge. At the bottom of the hill, she found a little cottage with a warm light in the window. The fox barked softly, and Luna knew she had found its home. She set the bell back in place, and the cottage door opened as if it had been waiting for her all along.
